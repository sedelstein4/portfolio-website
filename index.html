About

I've spent about six years building for the web and mobile, most of it in React and React Native. At Poliquicks I was a co-founder and COO: I built the app, led a 15-person team, ran prototyping and user testing in Figma, and handled partnerships and the roadmap.

Before that I spent two years with FoodCorps and AmeriCorps in DC public schools, running nutrition programs and a cafeteria feedback process across more than 100 schools. I've also worked as a deals analyst at Wirecutter, coordinated a mutual-aid food-sharing program, and led a campus group working on food insecurity. I'm happiest working on things that sit between technology, food and community.
